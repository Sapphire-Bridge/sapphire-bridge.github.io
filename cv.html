Profile

I care about how knowledge works — in logic, language, representation, and now in language models. Since 2018, I have pursued that question through philosophy, provability theory, and vagueness. Since late 2025, I have developed it into AI-enabled ML research, using AI itself to generate and test empirical evidence about how meaning-like behavior is governed inside transformers and whether mechanistic explanations of that behavior survive intervention.

Research Experience
Independent ML Researcher
Late 2025 – present

Developed Appearance of Meaning (AoM), a framework for identifying which internal states causally govern controlled meaning-like behavior in transformer language models.

Developed Mechanics of Meaning (MoM), a framework for testing whether proposed feature-level explanations preserve that behavior under matched intervention.

Established a sufficiency result showing that purely distributional transformer states can causally govern a controlled meaning-like competence profile, and a limitation result showing that strong reconstruction fidelity alone does not validate sparse feature-level causal explanation.

Structure of representation: investigated foundational conditions under which representational structures — formal, linguistic, conceptual — acquire explanatory power.
