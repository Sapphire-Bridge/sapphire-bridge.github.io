Research

Four papers around meaning and mechanistic interpretability, plus one shipped system.

A short index of what I've built. The arc moves from a philosophical seed about context-dependent meaning to causal tests on transformer LMs, and to a current limitation result on what SAE reconstruction fidelity actually buys.

01 Philosophical foundation

Context-Dependent Semantics in Large Language Models

Conceptual paper - philosophical framing.

Argues that decoder-only Transformer self-attention operationalizes a relational, context-dependent theory of meaning closer to Braun than to Kaplan. The empirical hook is that GPT-style embeddings exhibit low self-similarity and high anisotropy in deeper layers - the same token's representation shifts substantially with its context.

This frames the move from a philosophical claim about meaning to causal tests inside transformer models, which is what the next three papers carry out.

In GPT-2, upper-layer representations are more context-specific (Ethayarajh, 2019).

02 AoM - Paper 2

Appearance of Meaning (AoM)

Mathematics determines token, not meaning.
Under review at JoLLI (Journal of Logic, Language and Information).

Identifies internal states in transformer LMs that causally control meaning-like preference margins under context-swap activation patching, validated with sham-controlled comparisons. Across GPT-2 and Qwen2.5 checkpoints, donor-directed effects are structured and depth-localized; matched sham interventions sit near zero.

In the fixed-depth target-specificity test, all seven non-GPT-2 models had positive target-span contrast estimates; the 95% interval for Llama-3.2-1B included zero. These local comparisons do not establish global token-atomic exclusivity.

Structured, depth-localized donor-directed effects with near-zero sham baselines across GPT-2 and Qwen2.5.

03 MoM - Paper 3

Mechanics of Meaning (MoM)

Mechanism determines computation, not basis.
Completed manuscript.

Tests whether already-localized contextual control is better recovered in the raw residual stream or in a sparse learned feature basis. Compares matched raw residual and SAE interventions at shared resid_post sites in Gemma 2 2B across disambiguation, counterfactual-preference, and discourse-coherence tasks.

On DISAMB at L4, SAE patching has higher RMS-based effect-efficiency; its larger donor-directed effect remains directional, with the paired 95% interval including zero. Projection controls are documented, but the SVD construction does not support a clean comparison at equal data-determined rank.

The L4 advantage is in RMS-based effect-efficiency; the paired donor-directed effect difference remains uncertain. The finding is layer- and task-dependent.

04 Limitation - Paper 4 - CRR

Geometric Fidelity Does Not Certify Behavioral Preservation

State-reconstruction fidelity alone does not certify intervention-effect preservation.
Earlier draft on GitHub; description clarified here.

Tests whether error-preserving SAE delta patching preserves the effect of raw donor-state replacement. Raw patching replaces the receiver state with the donor state; SAE delta patching adds the difference between their SAE reconstructions to the receiver, preserving its reconstruction residual. CRR is the mean SAE effect divided by the mean raw effect. In Gemma 3 4B, state-reconstruction cosine is 0.997 at L4 and 0.998 at L8, with relMSE 0.0072 / 0.0067 and FVU 0.137 / 0.060. The corresponding CRR estimates are 0.621 (95% CI 0.263–0.954) and 0.974 (0.749–1.320). The L8 point estimate is close to raw; equivalence was not established.

An uncentered SVD projection comparison gives an L4 paired projection-minus-raw estimate of +0.013 (95% CI −0.217 to +0.247); this interval does not establish equivalence. The projection is fitted leave-one-pair-out on the evaluation distribution, while the SAE is frozen and trained elsewhere. It also includes directions beyond the data-supported rank. This is an implementation-specific comparison, not a clean comparison at equal data-determined rank, and it does not isolate a cause of the SAE effect loss.

Five residual-stream layers were tested (4, 5, 8, 11, 16). In this profile, FVU does not order observed CRR: L4 has the second-best FVU (0.137) and second-lowest CRR (0.621), while L5 has worse FVU (0.180) and higher CRR (0.908). L11 has lower recovery than L4, alongside worse reconstruction. Paired SAE-minus-raw at L4: −0.309 [−0.636, −0.027]; the strict-gate sensitivity interval includes zero. Full profile and limits.
